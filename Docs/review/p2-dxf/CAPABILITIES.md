## 7. What to port, and what to leave (ranked)

Measured basis: `SCOREBOARD.md` beside this file. On the 32 real DXF files ours names 70 rooms and his 16, ours leaves 0
crossings and his 57, his refuses 16 files; on his own fixture his is right and ours is wrong. So: **keep our reader, take
his good ideas into it** — each as a small change to `src/import`, measured on the formats scoreboard (no file worse) and
the 20 built-in round trips.

**Port (most worth it first):**
1. **Undo of an import, and a question first.** Ours: a DXF dropped on a drawing replaces it, nothing asked, Undo greyed
   out (measured). His: "Replace your current drawing with this DXF? Ctrl+Z restores your drawing." and one Undo brings
   it back with its picture. Port the idea (`dxf/studioState.ts trackImport`): the import is one undo step in our
   `model.ts` (`load-unit` keeps the history instead of starting fresh), for every architect's file, not only DXF.
2. **The DXF read off the page's own thread, with a limit that says why.** Ours froze on the 23.6 MB file for 5
   minutes; his refused it in 0.3 s. Port as ours does IFC (`import/ifc.worker.ts`: progress + Cancel), plus his plain
   sentences for "too big / too many entities / binary DXF" (`dxf/parse.ts` limits, `parseDxfBytes`).
3. **Text decoding**: his `plainMtext` reads `\U+XXXX` (a Bangla room name in an older DXF comes out as শয়ন কক্ষ; ours
   leaves `\U+09B6…`) and keeps escaped braces; ours reads `%%c %%d %%p` (Ø ° ±), his does not. Merge both into our
   `stripMtext`; his two MTEXT tests come with it.
4. **Fix a wrong scale by hand.** Ours has no way to re-read a DXF at another unit (S on a drawing with no picture lines
   up a picture): a wrongly guessed file stays wrong. His dialog's "1 DXF unit equals … metres" and "real length of the
   white wall" (`dxf/upload.ts scaleReference`). In ours: one row in "What was read from the file" ("Read in: millimetres
   ▾", or click a wall and type its length) that reads the file again, like the layer list does.
5. **Plain-line door / window symbols** on a door or window layer (a straight line or a narrow rectangle across a gap:
   `buildUnit.ts openingMarks` / `polylineOpening`): one more kind of evidence for an opening, after our swings, blocks and
   glass lines. His fixture's door and window need it.
6. **Single-line walls next to a floor outline** (his fixture): not his code — our `roomPolygons` mode should not start on
   one outline loop, and single lines should still be read in that mode. Add his fixture to our `dxf.test.ts`.
7. **Layer 0 inside a block takes the insert's layer** (CAD's own rule; `dxf/parse.ts add`): small, his test comes with it;
   measure on the corpus that nothing gets worse.
8. **A wall made of one door / window block** (all glass, all slider) outside Plotline's own export — not his code either;
   the plain-CAD flats show it is where ours loses rooms (the shot `builtin-type-a.neutral.dxf.png`; `plan2d.ts`: `if (!w && jambs && d.own)`). Try it without `d.own` and measure the corpus.

**Take as an idea, rebuild ours:**
- **Several drawings on one sheet** (`dxf/drawings.ts`): the need is real (`Docs/PLAN_DRAWING_SETS.md` Stage 1), but his
  distance rule split 5 single plans and was never tried on a real multi-plan sheet. Build it into Stage 1 with the view
  titles; his grid union-find is a fine starting point.
- **Layer roles remembered by what the layer holds** (`dxf/upload.ts learnedLayers`): safer than ours (name only); worth it
  once the founder's architects' files show a layer name meaning two things.
- **"Check role" on an unsure layer**: our layer list could mark the layers Plotline could not place the same way.

**Leave:**
- His wall engine (`extractWallRuns`, `joinWallGaps`, every unpaired line a 12.7 cm wall, every gap a guessed door): 2–7×
  the walls, 875 vs 187 loose ends and 57 vs 0 crossings on the real files; on the plain-CAD flats 269 crossings.
- His layer guess (English names only, "> 4 lines and no text → walls") and his names rule (known room words only).
- One review row per unsupported entity, ignored layer and guessed gap (3 072 rows on 32 files): against the founder's
  "fewer warnings, never hide them".
- His dialog as a step before every file (old dark look, not `Docs/DESIGN.md`; 2–3 steps per file where ours needs none).
- Import one inferred flat: the founder's rule is one whole floor per drawing; each flat opens on its own afterwards.
- Paper space as a drawing: rare in plans.
- His benchmark: one made-up file. Keep the formats scoreboard; add his fixture as a test.
- The dependency: dxf-parser 1.1.2 is already ours.

**Neither does:** binary DXF; old ANSI code pages (`$DWGCODEPAGE`); splitting a sheet by its view titles; a clean
plain-CAD flat fully (ours 78 of 116 rooms named, his 72).

### Raster input & walls

| Change in plain words | Where in the fork | His test | Ours (bb23229) | Proof that decides |
|---|---|---|---|---|
