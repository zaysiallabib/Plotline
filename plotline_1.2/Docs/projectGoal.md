# Plotline — 9+/10 Business Plan

## 1. The product

**Plotline = the collaborative operating system between architect and homeowner.**

Not a rendering tool.

The core object is a **live, editable digital building**.

It understands:

**walls → rooms → doors → windows → dimensions → furniture → materials → versions → comments → approvals.**

AI helps create and modify that model.

---

## 2. Kill Weakness #1 — Automatic architectural interpretation

Don't require architects to rebuild their projects manually.

### Input

Architect uploads:

* PDF floor plans
* CAD/BIM files
* elevations
* dimensions
* material schedules

Plotline extracts the structure.

### Example

Architect uploads a floor plan.

Plotline says:

> **Detected**
>
> 14 rooms
> 38 walls
> 12 doors
> 16 windows
> 2 floors
> Scale: 1:100
> Confidence: 94%

Architect verifies/corrects anything wrong.

**One-click → interactive building.**

### Important:

Do **not** promise 100% automatic conversion.

Use:

> **AI-assisted import + human verification**

That makes the product commercially realistic.

---

# 3. Kill Weakness #2 — Don't become "3D viewer + comments"

Every comment must belong to an **actual object**.

### Example

Homeowner walks into kitchen.

Clicks the island.

> **Change request #31**
>
> "Make this 20cm narrower and move it 30cm toward the window."

Plotline knows:

```text
Object: Kitchen Island
Current: 240 × 100 cm
Requested: 240 × 80 cm
Movement: +30 cm
Status: Pending
```

Architect opens the request.

**Accept → Reject → Edit**

Accepting creates:

**Version 7 → Version 8**

Now everyone sees exactly what changed.

That is your moat.

---

# 4. Kill Weakness #3 — Sell to businesses, not homeowners

Your paying customer should initially be:

### **Architectural / interior-design / developer firms**

They give Plotline to their clients.

Business value:

> **Fewer misunderstandings + fewer revision cycles + faster approvals.**

### Example

A firm currently has:

**Client → WhatsApp → architect → render → client → WhatsApp → architect → render**

Plotline becomes:

**Client → Plotline → exact spatial change → architect → approve → done**

If Plotline reduces a firm's average project from **8 client revision cycles → 5**, that's a financial product, not a toy.

---

# 5. The killer use case

### Project: $200,000 house

Architect uploads the actual plans.

Plotline creates the interactive building.

Homeowner enters.

### Homeowner:

> "This bedroom feels too small."

They select the bedroom wall and drag it **30cm**.

Plotline immediately shows:

**Before:** 12.4m²
**After:** 14.1m²

But Plotline also warns:

> ⚠️ Bathroom reduced by 0.8m²
> ⚠️ Door position affected
> ⚠️ Minimum corridor width maintained

Homeowner clicks:

**Create proposal**

Architect receives:

> **Proposal #14**
>
> Move Bedroom Wall 30cm.
>
> Affected objects: 4
> Structural review required: Yes.

Architect adjusts it properly and approves.

---

### Then homeowner says:

> "I want darker floors and cream walls."

Plotline changes the **actual materials**, not a fake AI image.

They walk around.

> "Actually, make the curtains beige."

Done.

---

### Then:

> "I don't like this window."

They click the actual window.

> "Move 40cm left."

Architect sees exactly which window.

No screenshot.

No WhatsApp explanation.

No guessing.

---

# 6. AI's role

AI becomes the **interface**, not the product.

User can say:

> "Make the living room feel bigger."

Plotline analyzes the actual model and proposes changes.

AI can also answer:

> "What changes have I requested?"

> "What changed since Version 4?"

> "Which requests haven't been approved?"

> "Will moving this wall affect anything?"

So AI makes Plotline **easier**, while the structured building model makes Plotline **valuable**.
